GOD'SGIFT — BURGUNDY PORTFOLIO

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP folder.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the website in your browser.
4. The opening door screen is the first page. Click "Enter My World" to open the doors and reveal the portfolio.

FILES
- index.html: website content and the door intro
- style.css: burgundy, blush, cream and gold design
- script.js: door-opening animation and skip-intro button
- assets/profile-photo.jpg: the profile photo you supplied
